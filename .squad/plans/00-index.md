| [foundation](foundation/00-overview.md) | The cross-cutting TF track (TF-01…TF-05, SCRUM-12…SCRUM-16) every feature story sits on. Sequenced first by the roadmap, built incrementally across 01–24, so its plans are **closure stories**: verify what shipped, fix what is wrong, resolve the criteria nobody ran. Opens with localisation and RTL (25, SCRUM-12) — seven of nine BRD criteria already pass; the work is making the language switch actually mirror the layout instead of leaving Arabic text in an LTR frame until the agent force-kills the app, taking the resolved language back out of the TanStack Query cache, and running the four verifications (cold-start flash, icon mirroring, locale-aware numbers/dates, and that the logical-property lint rule *fires*). Fixes both by shape and glyph rather than by hue, since neither is solvable inside a pale-pastel dark palette. Adds the repo's first `scripts/` dir and an `npm run contrast` gate, and closes the three chrome surfaces the token layer cannot reach — OS status bar, navigator background, dark splash. | 25, 26 |
